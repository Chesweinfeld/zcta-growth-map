    What “2011 to 2024” means. These are the names of two Census datasets, not two years. “2011” is the ACS 5-year release built from data collected January 2007 through December 2011; “2024” is the release covering January 2020 through December 2024. Each describes an area across its whole 60-month window rather than on any single date — the Census Bureau calls these period estimates and states they “do not represent a fixed date.” They are also not an average of five separate yearly figures: the five years are pooled and processed as one dataset. The two windows do not overlap, which is what makes comparing them meaningful; their starts and ends are 13 years apart. There is no single-year alternative at this geography — ZIP Code Tabulation Areas are not published in the ACS 1-year product at all, at any population size.

    Source: U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 5-year estimates (B01003, B25001) and 2020 ZCTA boundaries. Percentages are estimates with sampling error; small areas are the least certain.
